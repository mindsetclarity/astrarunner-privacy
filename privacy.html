Astra Runner: Divine Chariot: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

Astra Runner: Divine Chariot ("the game") is an offline endless runner for Android, published by Mindset Clarity. This policy explains what the game does with your data. In short, it collects none.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal or device data. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases. It does not ask for the internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere.

What stays on your phone

Your progress (best scores, in-game currency, vehicles, upgrades and settings) is saved in a file inside the game's own storage on your device. It never leaves your phone. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If the game ever starts collecting data, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
