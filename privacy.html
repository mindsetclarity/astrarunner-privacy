Astra Runner: Divine Chariot: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Astra Runner: Divine Chariot ("the game") is an endless runner for Android, published by Mindset Clarity. This policy explains what the game does with your data. In short, the game plays entirely offline; the only data that leaves your phone is what the world leaderboards need, sent to Unity Gaming Services, and you can turn that off.

World leaderboards (Unity Gaming Services)

The game's world leaderboards run on Unity Gaming Services, provided by Unity Technologies. When the game starts, it signs in to Unity anonymously: there is no account to create and no email or password is asked for. On first launch you choose a game name for the world board, or let the game roll one; it need not be your real name.

While the world board is on, Unity receives:

  • an anonymous player ID that Unity creates for this installation of the game;
  • the game name you chose (3 to 16 letters or numbers), shown next to your scores; it is sent even while the world board is off, so it is ready if you turn the board back on;
  • your best score on each difficulty, which the game sends to the leaderboard for that difficulty;
  • technical data Unity's software needs to run the service (such as the game's version and your device's IP address during the connection).

Other players see your game name and best scores on the world board. This data is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection and is handled under Unity's Privacy Policy.

Leaving the board: in Settings, turn WORLD BOARD off. The game stops sending scores and asks Unity to delete your scores from every world board; if you are offline, it asks again until Unity confirms. You can turn it back on at any time. Uninstalling the game ends the anonymous player ID on your phone; to have scores from an old installation removed, contact us below.

Reporting a name: on the WORLD tab you can tap another player's name to report it. The game then sends Unity that player's ID and the name as shown, together with your own anonymous player ID, and hides that name on your phone. Reports are kept in Unity Gaming Services until we review them; we may change or remove a reported name or its scores. Names are also checked on your phone against a list of offensive words before they are sent.

We never sell this data and do not use it for advertising.

What the game does not collect

The game has no accounts of its own, no ads, no analytics of its own and no in-app purchases. Apart from the world leaderboards described above, it never sends your data anywhere.

What stays on your phone

Your progress (best scores, in-game currency, vehicles, upgrades and settings) is saved in a file inside the game's own storage on your device, together with a copy of the last leaderboard you viewed. It never leaves your phone. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If the game starts collecting any other data, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
