Astra Runner: Divine Chariot: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Astra Runner: Divine Chariot ("the game") is an endless runner for Android, published by Mindset Clarity. This policy explains what the game does with your data. In short, the game plays entirely offline and collects nothing itself; the only data that leaves your phone goes to Google, and only if you use Google Play Games.

Google Play Games (world leaderboards)

The game uses Google Play Games Services for its world leaderboards. Signing in is optional: the whole game plays the same without it.

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, Google receives:

  • your Play Games player ID and gamer name (and avatar), so your scores can appear on the leaderboards;
  • your best score on each difficulty, which the game sends to the leaderboard for that difficulty;
  • diagnostics and analytics that Google's Play Games software collects to keep itself stable.

This data is sent to Google over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. Whether other players can see your scores depends on your Play Games profile's visibility setting. You can delete your Play Games data, including your scores, from your Play Games profile.

The game itself has no server. It does not receive, store or share this data anywhere else, and never sells it.

What the game does not collect

The game has no accounts of its own, no ads, no analytics of its own and no in-app purchases. Apart from Google Play Games as described above, it never sends your data anywhere.

What stays on your phone

Your progress (best scores, in-game currency, vehicles, upgrades and settings) is saved in a file inside the game's own storage on your device, together with a copy of the last leaderboard you viewed. It never leaves your phone. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If the game starts collecting any other data, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
